# Hi, I'm Amir
 
**Junior Data Analyst · Middle East Historian** · Brandenburg, Germany
 
I work with messy, multilingual and fast-changing data – from Persian-language Telegram channels to on-chain
transactions – and I document how every result was reached, including its limits. My background is in Iranian,
Islamic and Jewish studies. Python, SQL and Power BI are how I apply a historian's source criticism to data.
 
## What I bring
 
- **Source criticism:** I check where data comes from and what it can and cannot show. I state uncertainty
  instead of making results look stronger than they are.
- **Regional and language knowledge:** Iran and the wider Middle East; Persian (native), German (fluent).
- **End-to-end work:** collecting data (APIs), cleaning and validating it, modelling it (star schema,
  PostgreSQL), analysing it and presenting it (Streamlit, Power BI, Dune dashboards).
